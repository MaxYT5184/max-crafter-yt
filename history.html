Channel History

My YouTube Journey

Here's a timeline of key moments in my YouTube journey - I might not remember all the exact dates, but here are the highlights!

The Beginning - 2021

Uploaded my first funny gaming compilation video. Was just having fun with friends and decided to share it!

First 100 Subscribers

Reached my first 100 subscribers! Started doing more regular uploads and found my style of comedy gaming content.

First Roblox Stream - Early 2022

Started my first Roblox live stream after viewers kept asking for it. It was chaotic and hilarious!

200 Subscribers Milestone

Hit 200 subscribers! Started a weekly "Friday Night Chaos" Roblox stream series.

300 Subscribers

Reached 300 subscribers. Began collaborating with other small creators for multiplayer chaos videos.

400 Subscribers - Late 2022

Hit 400 subscribers! Started a Discord server for the community to hang out and suggest games.

500 Subscriber Special

Celebrated 500 subscribers with a 24-hour live stream marathon featuring viewer-suggested games.

600 Subscribers - Early 2023

Reached 600 subscribers! Started the "Saturday Subscriber Special" stream series.

700 Subscribers Milestone

Hit 700 subscribers! Launched this website to better connect with my audience.

Today - 719 Subscribers

Currently at 719 subscribers and counting! Working toward 1,000 for an epic celebration stream.
